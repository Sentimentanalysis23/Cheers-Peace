A great celebration is felt just as much as it is seen. At Cheers & Peace, our decor philosophy revolves around storytelling through ambiance. We don't just place flowers; we architect environments. 

For a recent high-profile wedding, we transformed an empty warehouse into a blooming, enchanted forest using over 10,000 fresh orchids, intelligent warm-white lighting, and custom-built reflective pathways. We believe that true luxury lies in the details—the exact shade of the draping, the scent of the room, and the seamless flow of the guest experience.
