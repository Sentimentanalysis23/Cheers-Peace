When it comes to large-scale events, the stage is the heart of the experience. Our production team specializes in end-to-end stage setups, including structural rigging, dynamic LED walls, and concert-grade audio-visual arrays. 

For recent outdoor concerts, we had to account for high wind loads, designing reinforced aluminum trusses that could safely hold massive speaker line-arrays while looking incredibly sleek. It’s this marriage of heavy-duty engineering and aesthetic design that sets Cheers & Peace apart in the live event industry.
