Building for the Aero India show requires extreme precision, strict security clearances, and world-class structural integrity. This year, Cheers & Peace took on the challenge of designing and fabricating pavilions for over 12 leading global brands in the aviation and defence sectors. 

Our team worked around the clock, deploying hundreds of tons of steel, custom lighting trusses, and bespoke branding elements to ensure every stall stood out on the global stage. The result was a flawless execution that left our clients thrilled and visitors amazed.
